TITLE: Tax Deductions, Exemptions, Rebates, and Regime Choices

An EXEMPTION generally removes an eligible amount or receipt from taxable income under a rule. A DEDUCTION reduces income or a specified base after eligibility conditions are met. A REBATE reduces tax payable when the taxpayer and income meet the relevant conditions. These are different ideas.

Common topics include standard deduction, house-rent allowance, home-loan interest, Section 80C items such as certain PF, PPF, ELSS, and life-insurance payments, and Section 80D health-insurance payments. Eligibility, limits, documentation, timing, and regime availability can change; a product being popular does not make it automatically deductible.

The new tax regime is the default for many individual taxpayers under current guidance, while eligible taxpayers may choose the old regime subject to the applicable procedure. Non-business and business/profession cases can have different switching rules. Identify the correct year and eligible deductions before comparing.

Never buy an investment or insurance product solely for a tax label. This is educational information, not a recommendation to choose a regime or claim a deduction.
