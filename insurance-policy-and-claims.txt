TITLE: Life and Health Insurance: Coverage, Exclusions, Claims, and Policy Documents

Insurance transfers defined risks to an insurer in exchange for a premium, subject to the policy contract. LIFE INSURANCE generally pays a benefit on events defined in the policy. HEALTH INSURANCE may reimburse eligible medical expenses or pay a defined benefit, depending on the product.

SUM ASSURED is commonly used for life insurance; SUM INSURED is commonly used for health or general insurance. Check the policy schedule, coverage, exclusions, waiting periods, deductibles, co-pay, sub-limits, room-rent limits, network hospitals, claim process, renewal, and cancellation terms.

A PRE-EXISTING DISEASE, waiting period, exclusion, or non-disclosure can affect a claim. Disclose information accurately and retain proposal forms, policy documents, premium receipts, and claim correspondence. A Customer Information Sheet summarises important policy features.

Life cover and health cover solve different risks. A savings or investment product is not automatically a substitute for insurance. Money Genie explains terms and checklists but cannot decide whether a claim will be paid.
