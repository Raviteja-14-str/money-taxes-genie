# Money Genie content roadmap

Updated: 2026-08-15

The current first pass covers 20 educational files across salary, budgeting, banking, deposits, loans, credit, UPI safety, tax workflow, deductions, GST, investing, mutual funds, insurance, EPF, NPS, PPF, scams, and complaint awareness.

The following areas are intentionally not represented as current advice yet:

- Capital-gains tax, dividends, ESOP/RSU, crypto/VDA, foreign income, NRI rules, and tax-loss concepts.
- GST registration, invoicing, input-tax credit, returns, e-way bills, and small-business compliance.
- Real estate purchase/rent, home loans, stamp duty, registration, and property tax.
- Education loans, gold, bonds, government securities, REITs/InvITs, and commodities.
- Small-business bookkeeping, cash-flow statements, working capital, and business taxes.
- Consumer grievance workflows with current regulator portal links.
- Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, and other Indian-language versions with terminology review.
- Live rates, product comparisons, market prices, due dates, and calculators. These need a dated data-refresh layer or deterministic code, not static model memory.

For every next topic, add an authoritative source, a checked date, a small set of evaluation questions, and a safety test. A topic is ready only when the local and Bedrock retrieval paths identify the correct source and the model refuses unsupported personalised advice.
