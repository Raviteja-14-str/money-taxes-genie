TITLE: Loans, EMI, Interest, Credit Scores, and Responsible Borrowing

PRINCIPAL is the amount borrowed. INTEREST is the cost charged for using the money. An EMI usually contains both interest and principal. A longer tenure may reduce the monthly payment while increasing total interest, so compare total repayment as well as EMI.

SECURED borrowing uses an asset or other security, while UNSECURED borrowing generally does not. A credit card is a payment and borrowing product, not free money. Paying only the minimum due can leave interest accruing on the unpaid balance.

When comparing a loan, look beyond the advertised rate: processing fees, insurance, late charges, foreclosure terms, floating-rate reset rules, taxes, and total amount payable all matter. Do not borrow to invest just because a projection shows a possible return.

A CREDIT REPORT records borrowing and repayment information supplied by lenders. A credit score is a summary generated by a credit bureau's model; it is not a measure of a person's worth or a guarantee of approval.
