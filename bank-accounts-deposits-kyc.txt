TITLE: Bank Accounts, Deposits, KYC, Nomination, and Deposit Insurance

A savings account is commonly used for payments and accessible savings. A current account is generally designed for frequent business transactions. A fixed deposit locks money for an agreed period and rate, while a recurring deposit accepts regular contributions; products and terms differ by institution.

KYC means Know Your Customer. Banks and regulated entities use identity and address information to meet legal and anti-money-laundering requirements. KYC is not a reason to share passwords, PINs, OTPs, card numbers, or account credentials with another person.

NOMINATION tells an institution who should receive or claim assets through its process after the account holder's death. It is useful but does not replace a complete estate plan or settle every legal question. Keep nominations and contact details current.

Before opening a deposit, compare the interest calculation, tenure, premature-closure rules, renewal instruction, tax treatment, liquidity, and institution safety. A high headline rate may not suit a short-term cash need.

DICGC deposit insurance is subject to its rules and limits. The current DICGC guide states that each depositor is insured up to ₹5 lakh for principal and interest in the same right and capacity at an insured bank. The cover is not a promise that every financial product is insured; verify the bank and the latest DICGC guidance.

Never let Money Genie collect or store bank login information, OTPs, card PINs, or full account numbers. For a real account issue, use the bank's official channel and its complaint process.

This is educational information and does not recommend a bank or deposit.
