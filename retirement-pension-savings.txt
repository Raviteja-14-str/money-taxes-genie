TITLE: EPF, EPS, EDLI, NPS, PPF, and Retirement Planning Basics

EPF is a contributory provident-fund scheme administered by EPFO for eligible covered employment. EPFO also administers EPS, a pension scheme, and EDLI, an insurance scheme. Salary slips may show employee and employer contributions separately.

UAN is used to connect a member's EPF member IDs across employment. Keep the profile, nomination, and employment records accurate. Transfer and withdrawal rules are specific; do not treat a social-media shortcut as an official procedure.

NPS is a defined-contribution pension system regulated by PFRDA. Its value depends on contributions, asset allocation, market performance, charges, and exit rules. NPS is not a guaranteed return product.

PPF is a government small-savings scheme available through authorised banks and post offices. It is long-term and has contribution, lock-in, loan, withdrawal, interest, and tax rules that should be checked against current official guidance.
